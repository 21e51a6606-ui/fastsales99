How it works

  1. Pair a WhatsApp number (QR pairing is simulated in this demo).
  2. Inbound messages arrive via POST /dashboard/whatsapp/webhook.
  3. Replies from the live inbox are logged and sent through the configured provider.
  4. AI agents attached to a product can auto-respond to inbound messages.
